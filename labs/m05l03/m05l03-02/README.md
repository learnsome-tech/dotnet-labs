## Goal

You can generate and expose an OpenAPI document with AddOpenApi and MapOpenApi.

In the lesson: The registration adds the generator to the service collection. Mapping the document adds an endpoint, normally under the development or documentation path chosen by the application. The built in support produces a standards based document but no visual interface. Keep the document endpoint behind the same environment and authorization decisions as the rest of your API, and make its route part of your operational documentation.
